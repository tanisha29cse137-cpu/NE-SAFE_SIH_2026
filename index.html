One platform. Four critical jobs.

The concept follows the complete disaster-management cycle.

🧠

Risk Assessment

Estimate flood and landslide risk from rainfall, river levels, terrain and historical information.

📢

Early Warning

Convert rising risk into clear, location-specific alerts for communities and authorities.

📸

AI Verification

Combine citizen text, images and location signals to increase confidence in emergency reports.

🚑

Smart Response

Support evacuation routing, shelter selection and rescue-resource prioritization.
